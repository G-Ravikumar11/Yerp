# P5 - Reports and integration

Backend: `models/store_reports.py`, `services/store_reports.py`, `services/store_integration.py`, `routers/store_reports.py`,
`documents/store/report_forms.py`, plus three additive hooks in existing modules (below). Frontend: `features/store/reports/`.
Tests: `backend/tests/store/test_p5_reports.py` and `test_p5_end_to_end.py` (skips itself until P1-P4 are merged).
Tables (new, made by `create_all`): `store_material_norm`, `store_saved_report`. Reports read the stock ledger and the balances and nothing else, so
what a report says always adds up to the stock the stores hold.

## Rights

`store.reports.view` runs every report and lists norms and saved reports; `store.reports.create` sets norms and saves filters. A member of staff's
reports cover only the stores they are assigned to (`store.admin` and the account holder see the whole company); another company's projects and
stores answer 404. Reports are views, not documents, so `approve`, `post` and `cancel` have nothing to do here.

## Reports (`GET /api/store/reports/{report}`; add `/export` for Excel or `/print` for PDF with the same filters)

| Report | Filters | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `valuation` | `store_id`, `as_on`, `category`, `q`, `include_zero` | Quantity, average rate and value per store and item - today from the balances, on a past date from the ledger up to it. Totals by category |
| `movement` | `store_id`, `from_date`, `to_date`, `q` | Opening, the net movement in five groups (receipts, returned to supplier, issues net of returns, transfers, adjustments/wastage/scrap) and closing, per store and item. Every row closes to the valuation |
| `consumption` | `store_id`, `project_id`, `from_date`, `to_date`, `group_by` = project / cost_head / work_order / boq_item / item | What was consumed, net of returns and reversals |
| `norm-variance` | `project_id` (required), `tolerance_pct` | For each BOQ item with a norm: what it should have used, what the stores issued to it, the difference and `OVER` / `UNDER` / `OK` |
| `slow-moving` | `store_id`, `days` (default 90) | Stock that has not moved for at least that many days, and what is tied up |
| `reorder` | `store_id` | Items at or below their reorder level, with a suggested quantity (to the maximum, or twice the reorder level) |

A reversal is a ledger row of the same movement type in the opposite column, so summing signed quantities and values nets it with the original;
nothing is filtered out. `txn_date` of a reversal is the day it was made, so a reversal in a later period nets in that later period.

## Material consumption (what is "cost")

Cost is the movements that consume stock: `ISSUE_OUT`, `SUBCON_ISSUE_OUT`, `WASTAGE_OUT`, `SCRAP_OUT`, less what comes back from site (`ISSUE_RETURN_IN`,
`SUBCON_RETURN_IN`). Receipts, transfers (even between projects), purchase returns and count adjustments are not consumption. Values are the ledger's own
(issues at the store's average rate of the day). The dimensions are those the ledger entry carries: project, cost head, work order, BOQ item.

## Norms

`POST/PUT/DELETE /norms`, `GET /norms?project_id=|boq_item_id=`: for a BOQ item and an item, `qty_per_unit` (of the item's base unit, per 1 unit of the
BOQ item's quantity) and a `wastage_pct` allowance. Theoretical requirement = BOQ quantity x qty per unit x (1 + wastage / 100):
`store_reports.get_theoretical_requirement(db, boq_item_id, item_id)` returns it, or None when there is no norm. One norm per pair; the BOQ item must be
a priced item of the company and the item a stock item. `GET /pick/boq-items?project_id=` lists a project's priced BOQ items.

## Saved reports

`GET /api/store/reports` lists them (this is the slice's front door), `POST /saved` keeps a report and its filters under a name (the same name replaces
it), `DELETE /saved/{id}` removes one.

## What the rest of the ERP reads (`services/store_integration.py`)

* `get_project_material_cost(db, project_id, from_date, to_date)` - the cross-slice contract: consumption by cost head, work order, BOQ item and item,
  net of returns and reversals. `GET /project-cost/{project_id}` shows the same figures.
* **Three additive hooks** (P5 may edit existing modules; each only adds a key and changes nothing that was there):
  * `services/projects.py` `job_costing` adds `store_material`: the net value the stores have issued to the job, shown **beside** the bills and **not
    added** to `total_cost` (the stock was bought on a bill already, so adding it would count it twice). The board reads it in one query for all jobs.
  * `services/procurement.py` `purchase_order_to_dict` (the single-order view only) adds `store_receipt`: per line, what the store has received,
    accepted and rejected and what is pending, from `store_inward.get_po_receipt_summary`.
  * `services/subcontract_billing.py` `sub_bill_dict` (the detailed view) adds `store_material_to_recover`: what the store issued to the bill's
    contractor that is chargeable and not yet back, from `store_outward.get_subcon_issues`. It is informational: it is **not** entered as a recovery on
    the bill - the legacy `material_recoveries` mechanism (which belongs to the old stock module) is untouched.

## Screens (`/store/hub/reports/...`)

One page per report with its filters, the result as a table, Excel and PDF of the same figures, and saving the filters under a name; and *Set norms*,
where a project's norms are listed, added, changed and removed.
