# P4 - Control

Backend: `models/store_control.py`, `services/store_control.py`, `routers/store_control.py`, `documents/store/control_forms.py`.
Frontend: `features/store/control/`. Tests: `backend/tests/store/test_p4_control.py`.
Tables (all new, made by `create_all`): `store_transfer`, `store_transfer_line`, `store_count`, `store_count_line`, `store_scrap`,
`store_scrap_line`, `store_control_log`.

## Documents

| Path key | Ledger `ref_doc_type` | Number | Moves stock |
|---|---|---|---|
| `transfer` | `TRANSFER` | `TRF/CS01/26-27/00001` | Dispatch (POST): `TRANSFER_OUT` at the source's average rate. Receive: `TRANSFER_IN` at the destination at that rate |
| `count` | `COUNT` | `COUNT/...` | Post: `ADJUST_IN` (surplus, at the average rate when the count started) / `ADJUST_OUT` (shortage, at the average) |
| `scrap` | `SCRAP` | `SCRAP/...` | Post: `SCRAP_OUT` or `WASTAGE_OUT` per line, at the average rate |

Lifecycle for all three: `DRAFT -> SUBMITTED -> APPROVED -> POSTED`, or `CANCELLED` from any earlier state. Only POSTED touches stock; only a
DRAFT is edited or deleted; a POSTED document is immutable - cancel it (which reverses its movements) and make a new one. Every change of status
is a row in `store_control_log`. Posting is one transaction: if anything cannot move, nothing does and the document keeps its status.

## Rights

`store.control.view` reads; `.create` makes, edits and deletes drafts, submits, starts a count and enters its sheet; `.approve` approves;
`.post` posts (and receives a transfer); `.cancel` cancels. `.reveal_count` shows the system quantity while a count is open.
`.approve_high_value` is needed to approve a document **worth 50,000 or more** (`HIGH_VALUE` in the service): a transfer (at the source's average
rates), a count (the total size of its differences, shortages and surpluses alike) or a scrap document. Staff only see and work in the stores
they are assigned to; another company's records answer 404.

## Transfers

* Source = the document's store; the destination (`to_store_id`) may be any active store of the company - sending needs access to the source only
  (`GET /pick/stores` lists all stores for the picker, because the shared lookup only offers a person's own). A transfer is visible from both ends.
* Submit checks the source holds the quantities (lines of an item add up); post checks again in the posting transaction.
* **Receiving** (`POST /transfer/{id}/receive`) needs `store.control.post` **and access to the destination store**, only works on a dispatched transfer
  that is still in transit, and works once. The full quantity is received; there is no partial receipt - correct a difference with a count.
* `inter_project` is true when the two stores belong to different projects (a store with no project counts as its own).
* Cancelling a dispatched transfer reverses the stock that moved; once received it reverses both ends and fails (nothing half-done) if the
  destination has already used the stock.
* `get_in_transit(db, project_id)` lists the dispatched, not yet received lines, valued at what they left at; with a project, the transfers that
  leave or arrive at one of its stores.

## Stock counts

1. **Create** (FULL = everything the store holds; PARTIAL = chosen items). 2. **Start** (`POST /count/{id}/start`) freezes the store - nothing can be
received, issued or transferred - and snapshots the books (quantity and average rate). 3. **Enter counts** (`PUT /count/{id}/sheet`, repeatable to
recount); an item the books do not have may be added to a full count with the rate it is worth. 4. **Submit** needs every item counted and a reason for
each difference. 5. **Approve**. 6. **Post** unfreezes the store and adjusts the books, in one transaction; it refuses if the books moved since the start.
Cancelling an open count unfreezes the store; cancelling a posted one reverses its adjustments. A store has one open count at a time.

**Blind counting**: until the count is submitted, `system_qty`, the variances and the value are hidden (`null`) from anyone without
`store.control.reveal_count`; the account holder and `store.admin` always see them. The printed count sheet never carries the system quantity.
`get_count_variances(db, store_id, from, to)` returns the lines of POSTED counts; a shortage is a negative variance.

## Wastage and scrap

Each line is SCRAP (which can record what it fetched) or WASTAGE (which may be charged to a cost head), with a required reason. A line may deal
with an open outward **write-off request** (`write-off request` raised when a returnable is lost): on posting it calls
`store_outward.close_writeoff_request`, and a document that did so cannot be cancelled afterwards (the request cannot be reopened).
`get_scrap_realisation(db, project_id, from, to)` lists posted SCRAP lines (not wastage): book value is what the ledger took the stock out at.

## API (`/api/store/control`)

`GET ""` (latest transfers); `GET /pick/stores`, `/pick/writeoffs?store_id=`; reports `GET /in-transit`, `/count-variances?store_id=`,
`/scrap-realisation`. For each kind (`transfer`, `count`, `scrap`): `GET /{kind}`, `GET /{kind}/export` (Excel), `POST /{kind}`, `GET|PUT|DELETE /{kind}/{id}`
(a count has no PUT), `GET /{kind}/{id}/print` (PDF), `POST /{kind}/{id}/{submit|approve|post|cancel}`; plus `POST /transfer/{id}/receive`,
`POST /count/{id}/start`, `PUT /count/{id}/sheet`.

## Screens (`/store/hub/control/...`)

Lists for transfers, counts and scrap; the document page (with Receive for transfers and the count sheet for counts); the forms; and the In transit,
Count differences and Scrap realised pages.
